# DL7YAL Webspace – Current Framework

Status: 2026-09-06
Purpose: Authoritative handoff/reference for the current DL7YAL company website concept before final implementation and legal completion.

## Delivery target

Build a complete upload-ready website package for DL7YAL.de. Do not deploy to IONOS and do not require IONOS credentials. Final delivery is a ZIP/package that can be uploaded to the existing IONOS webspace by the owner.

Preferred implementation: lean static HTML/CSS/JS unless a concrete site requirement later makes server-side functionality necessary. Keep external dependencies minimal and assets local where practical.

## Information architecture

Main navigation is fixed to:

- Home
- Über uns
- Software
- Referenzen
- Kontakt

Do not add `Wissen` or `Projekte` as main navigation items. Technical knowledge belongs contextually inside relevant pages/case studies. Projects are presented selectively as `Referenzen`.

Legal pages required before release:

- Impressum
- Datenschutz

Do not invent operator, register, tax, hosting, analytics, contact-form or other legally relevant details. Missing legal data must be collected from the owner in one consolidated pass before final release.

## Brand character

DL7YAL should feel:

- solide
- ehrlich
- authentisch
- technisch kompetent
- eigenständig
- besonders

`Besonders` must come from thoughtful products, quality, individuality and a recognizable own signature — not from loud or artificial presentation.

## Visual system

- Base: Midnight Blue / very dark navy
- Neutral elements: white / silver
- Accent: controlled electric blue / cyan
- Style: elegant, restrained, modern technical precision
- Premium quality without flashy gaming aesthetics
- Spacious layouts, high contrast, rounded/glass-like cards where useful
- Modern sans-serif typography
- Atmospheric nature/landscape imagery may communicate space, perspective and future
- Globe/network imagery may communicate connection/technology
- Avoid generic tech clipart and literal category illustrations
- Avoid survival/outdoor symbolism
- Referenzen hero: real computer/hardware/technology with subtle digital/network light structures; no compass, no RGB-gaming cliché, no generic datacenter

### Accent rule

Cyan/electric blue is reserved for controlled highlights such as:

- `TOMORROW`
- active navigation
- links
- calls to action

On Home the three value icons/labels `Fokus · Verbindung · Entwicklung` must all use the same white/silver treatment. The Entwicklung icon must not have a cyan/blue glow.

## Company/product philosophy

Competitive research is market awareness only, never a copying instruction. Products and presentation should be solid, clear, honest, individual and recognizable. Do not imitate competitors or reproduce their designs/content.

## Legal/privacy release workflow

Build design, content and technical implementation first. Before final release, identify the actual website services and then request only the legal data that is genuinely required.

Likely owner data may include, as applicable:

- legal operator/name
- serviceable address
- contact details
- legal form/status
- register data if applicable
- VAT/tax disclosure if legally applicable
- responsible person where legally required

Technical privacy review must establish actual use of:

- hosting
- contact form/email handling
- analytics
- cookies/local storage
- external fonts
- maps
- embeds/video/social media
- CDNs/external scripts
- CAPTCHA or anti-spam services

Prefer a data-minimizing implementation. Do not add analytics, tracking, cookies or external embeds merely by default. Cookie/consent UI should only be introduced when the actual implementation requires it.

The final legal pages must reflect the real deployed implementation. No claim of guaranteed legal compliance should be made without appropriate legal review.
